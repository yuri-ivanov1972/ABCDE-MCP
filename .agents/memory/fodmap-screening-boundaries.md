---
name: FODMAP screening boundaries
description: Product scope and safety rationale for dietary screening
---

FODMAP is the first dietary feature requested for this product. Treat it as ingredient screening, not a verified low-FODMAP rating or serving-size recommendation.

**Why:** The public catalogue provides ingredients and general analysis, not established laboratory FODMAP measurements or validated portion thresholds. Monash's public guidance explicitly says ingredient lists alone cannot establish FODMAP content because processing and serving size matter.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between ingredient concerns, insufficient information, and no listed concern whenever expanding rules or presenting results. A no-listed-concern result must never become a safety/certification claim. Do not use normal sugar totals, gluten-free branding or catalogue analysis scores as proof of FODMAP suitability, and do not infer any medical condition about the project owner.
